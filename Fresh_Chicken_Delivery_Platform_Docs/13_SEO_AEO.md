# SEO & AEO Specification

---

## 1. Local SEO

Primary market:
Mulbagal.

The website should consistently expose:
- Business name.
- Category.
- Service area.
- Contact information.
- Shop location.
- Business hours.
- Delivery policy.

Do not create fake city pages or keyword-stuffed pages.

---

## 2. Technical SEO

Implement:
- Title tags.
- Meta descriptions.
- Canonical URLs.
- XML sitemap.
- robots.txt.
- Open Graph.
- Twitter/X metadata where appropriate.
- Semantic headings.
- Clean slugs.
- Fast images.
- Responsive design.
- Internal linking.
- Structured data.

---

## 3. Structured Data

Potential schemas:
- LocalBusiness / appropriate subtype.
- Product.
- Offer.
- BreadcrumbList.
- FAQPage only where actual visible FAQ content exists.
- Review/AggregateRating only when implementation meets search-engine guidelines.

Never fabricate ratings/reviews.

---

## 4. AEO

Write concise factual answers to likely questions:

- Where does Fresh Chicken deliver?
- What is the minimum order?
- How much is delivery?
- What chicken cuts are available?
- Can chicken be customized?
- How long does delivery take?
- Which payment methods are available?
- Is COD available?
- What is the COD charge?

Answers should exist as useful visible content.

---

## 5. Performance

Targets:
- Fast LCP.
- Low layout shift.
- Optimized image sizes.
- Lazy-load below-the-fold imagery.
- Avoid huge JS bundles.

---

## 6. Content Strategy

Useful local content:
- Product guides.
- Chicken cut information.
- Freshness/quality practices.
- Ordering instructions.
- Delivery coverage.
- FAQs.

Do not publish thin AI-generated pages merely to increase URL count.
